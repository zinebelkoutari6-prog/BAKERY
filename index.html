Freshly Made for Our Community

North Star Bakery is a neighborhood bakery that makes fresh breads, pastries, and cakes for local customers and families. We believe that good food should feel homemade and bring people together.

Whether you need breakfast for the family, a special cake, or bread for an event, we are happy to help.
